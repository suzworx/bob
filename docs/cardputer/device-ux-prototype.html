Why don't I see…

…the listening mode change, or the full shortcut strip?
They belong to Ready. k, c, n, s, h and ? are read on Ready only; on any other screen they do nothing, with no toast. On WS RECONNECT the strip is just "GO try again": press GO (or wait for the automatic retry) and, once the server welcomes the device, Ready and its full strip come back. See the scenario "Device offline → reconnects → Ready".
…"Hey Bob"?
The default wake word is "Alexa" (ESPHome's model). "Hey Bob" is a custom-trained model and the backup: w in Ready switches between them, and the choice is kept on the device (include/wake_models.h).
…volume keys working in help or a list?
- = m work on Ready and while a reply is on its way, nowhere else.
…a mode picker or alarms through DND?
Not built. Switch to "Shipped + planned" to see the planned ones. Do not disturb today holds every announcement on the server, alarms included.

    Notes for review

    • Layout at 240x135: status line rows 0-10 (text at y 2, grey rule at y 10), content 11-123, shortcut strip rows 124-134 (a 2 bpp sprite: black, grey #b0b0b0 labels, yellow keys, cyan accents). Pairing screens hide both rows.
    • Strip timing (chrome.cpp): new text holds still 0.6 s, then moves at 100 px/s (elapsed-time position, drawn at most every 40 ms) if it is wider than 232 px, loops with a 36 px gap and holds still again each lap. Toasts (cyan, centred) and the volume bar cover it for 1.5 s; afterwards it starts at the beginning, holds still 0.6 s and scrolls again.
    • Keys inside [ ] are drawn yellow without the brackets, so "[m] mute" reads "m mute"; adjacent keys keep a space, so "[-][=] volume" reads "- = volume".
    • In Always awake the red mic dot and white mode label stay on at Ready: the microphone really runs, feeding only the on-device wake word model. The volume-key beep is silent then (earcons never play over a running mic).
    • If the wake word engine cannot start, the device falls back: toast "Wake word failed. Hold GO to talk", status "PTT (no wake)", the plain Ready strip, and it reports mode=ptt. Entering Always awake again retries.
    • Announcements show over Ready and help only. The server holds them while the device is busy or in DND; the device queues up to 3 more. A reminder chimes, is read out and closes 10 s after; timers and alarms use distinct candidate patterns in the selected Classic, Chime or Pulse style, for 30 seconds in this demo.
    • Follow-up window (follow_up.h, #392): after a reply in Always awake (not DND, not "PTT (no wake)") the listen earcon plays and the mic stays open 6 s: "Listening..." / "(say more, or wait)", strip "Listening... just talk". Speech opens a turn with no wake word and no earcon; silence closes it with its own follow-up-close cue and the reply card shows. Any key only closes it (x too); GO closes it and talks. At most 2 follow-ups per wake.
    • Timings of the server turn are approximate; the real reply waits for full STT, agent and TTS.
    • Settings (settings_ui.cpp): lists, confirmations and fields reuse the picker, a title-plus-lines screen and a framed field (a cyan cursor, the n/max counter, a red error line at y 96). A password shows each new character for 1 s, then *. Settings closes by itself after 2 minutes idle (Setup never does); the prototype does not model that timeout.
    • The pairing QR code is a stand-in pattern: the device draws a real, scannable code of the verification address (M5GFX qrcode()) in the same white 96 px box.
    • Demo Wi-Fi: HomeNet (password sunflower42), Pixel hotspot (hotspot42), Neighbour-2G (any password fails), Cafe Guest (open), CorpNet (enterprise, "not supported"). "Developer build" (Simulate) is the default m5stack-cardputer-v2-poc env; untick it for the release build, where Server is hidden until you type developer on About.
